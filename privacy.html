This Privacy Policy explains what information dumplingfish (a sole proprietorship) ("we", "us") collects when you play our games, and how we use and share it. It covers MahjongNook and MahjongNook Cards (each a "Game", together the "Games") on every platform where they are offered, including the Apple App Store, Google Play, Steam, the web, and Discord. Where something applies to only one Game, we say so.

MahjongNook: you can play offline without an account. Creating or signing in to an account is required for online play, social features, purchases, and cloud-saved progress.

MahjongNook Cards: you can play without signing in. When you do, the Game creates a random player identifier on your device, and the progress and items tied to it are kept on our servers. Signing in lets your progress follow you to other devices and is required for purchases.

1. Information we collect

Account & identity

  • Sign-in identifiers. When you create or sign in to an account we process a player identifier and, depending on the method you choose, an email address (email/password sign-in) or a third-party identifier from Google, Apple, Steam, or Discord. With Discord sign-in (MahjongNook Cards) we receive your Discord user ID and username. MahjongNook does not offer anonymous guest accounts; MahjongNook Cards keeps a random player identifier for players who have not signed in, as described above.
  • Display name. A name you choose, shown to other players in matches and social features.

Gameplay & profile

  • Progress and statistics (matches played, wins/losses, skill rating, daily-puzzle streaks, missions, battle-pass progress).
  • In-game economy state: balances of in-game currencies (coins and Jade), owned cosmetic items, and equipped customization.
  • Your in-game mail/gift inbox (reward receipts and announcements), including which entries you have read and which gifts you have claimed.
  • Settings and preferences you set in the Game.
  • This data is stored in your account's cloud save so it follows you across devices.
  • MahjongNook Cards: the moves of the hands you play. Our servers check them so results and rewards are fair, and they power match replays and hand review. Replays, saved matches, and settings are also kept on your device.

Purchases

  • When you buy a real-money item (e.g., a Jade pack or a Club subscription), the purchase and payment are handled by the platform store: Apple, Google, Valve/Steam, or Discord. We never receive or store your full payment-card details.
  • We receive and process the store's purchase receipt/transaction to verify it and grant your items, and we keep a record of the validated transaction (product, store, transaction id, and grant) for fulfillment, support, and fraud prevention.
  • In MahjongNook Cards on iOS, purchases are verified through RevenueCat, which receives your player identifier and the store's purchase records so we can confirm and grant what you bought.

Social features

  • Friends list, friend requests, online presence, and lobby invitations, if you use these features.
  • In-match expression uses a fixed set of preset emotes — the Game does not include free-form text chat, so we do not collect typed messages between players.

Multiplayer networking

  • MahjongNook Cards: online tables run on our servers, hosted by Cloudflare, which process network identifiers such as IP addresses to connect you. Other players never see your IP address.
  • MahjongNook: online matches connect through a relay service, which carries game actions between players. Network identifiers such as IP addresses are processed by the transport/relay provider to route traffic; the Game's design relays connections rather than exposing players' IP addresses directly to one another.

Rewarded ads (MahjongNook Cards, iOS app)

  • The iOS app may offer optional ads you can choose to watch for a small in-game reward. These ads are shown by Google AdMob, and we request non-personalized ads only, so they are not based on your activity in other apps or websites.
  • Google processes information such as your IP address and device information to deliver the ad, measure it, and prevent fraud. To confirm a reward, Google sends our server a one-time code that is not linked to your name or email.
  • No ads appear unless you choose to watch one, and the web and Discord versions show no ads.

Device & diagnostics

This section describes MahjongNook. MahjongNook Cards does not currently collect crash reports or analytics.

  • Crash and error reports. If the Game crashes or hits an error, we collect diagnostic data to fix it: app version, engine version, platform, device model, recent log output, and non-identifying context (whether you were signed in, and the current match's game variant and random seed so the issue can be reproduced). These reports do not include your email or payment data.
  • Service identifiers. Unity Gaming Services may assign technical identifiers (such as an installation id) used to operate the services and, where applicable, attribute transactions.
  • Gameplay analytics. We collect a small set of anonymous gameplay events (such as a match starting or ending, its game variant and duration, a mission or mail reward being claimed, and a purchase being completed) to understand how the Game is played and improve it. These events are tied to the technical service identifiers above, not to your name or email, and we do not use them for personalized advertising.

  • Run online matchmaking, multiplayer sessions, and social features.
  • Process and verify purchases and grant purchased items.
  • Maintain fair play, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms.
  • Diagnose crashes, debug, and improve stability and the player experience.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

If you are in the EEA or the UK, we process your personal data on the following legal bases: to perform our contract with you (creating and running your account, gameplay, cloud saves, matchmaking, social features, and processing purchases); our legitimate interests (keeping the Game secure, preventing fraud and cheating, understanding and improving how the Game is played, and providing support), weighed against your rights and freedoms; compliance with legal obligations (such as keeping records of transactions for tax and accounting); and, where we ask for it, your consent. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

4. Sharing & disclosure

  • We share information with the service providers above only as needed to run the Game.
  • Your display name, presence, and in-match activity are visible to other players you play or connect with.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Data retention

We keep account and gameplay data while your account is active. Purchase/transaction records are kept as long as needed for fulfillment, support, fraud prevention, and legal/accounting obligations. Diagnostic reports are retained for a limited period for debugging. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify the personal data associated with it, except records we must keep for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes.

To make a request, contact us at dumplingfishgames@gmail.com. You can also delete your account and its associated data at any time from within the Game (in MahjongNook, Account settings; in MahjongNook Cards, Profile, then Account, then Delete account), or by contacting us at the same address. Deleting an account does not cancel a subscription billed by an app store; cancel it in that store's settings. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
